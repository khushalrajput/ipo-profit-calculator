Example Calculation

Investment: ₹15,000  |  Actual IPO: ₹14,877  |  Profit: 22%  |  Tax: 20%

1. Unused Amount

₹15,000 − ₹14,877 = ₹123.00

2. Gross Profit

₹14,877 × 22% = ₹3,272.94

3. Tax

₹3,272.94 × 20% = ₹654.59

4. Profit After Tax

₹3,272.94 − ₹654.59 = ₹2,618.35

5. Profit Split (50/50)

₹2,618.35 ÷ 2 = ₹1,309.18 each

6. User A — Investor

₹1,309.18 (profit) + ₹123.00 (unused) = ₹1,432.18 settlement component

7. User B — IPO Applicant

₹14,877.00 (IPO principal) + ₹1,309.18 (profit) + ₹654.59 (tax) = ₹16,840.77 total IPO proceeds in B's account

The ₹14,877 IPO principal belongs to the original investment and is returned through B's IPO proceeds — it is not profit.

8. B Must Transfer to A

Since A invested ₹15,000 and all money is in B's account:

₹15,000.00 (return investment) + ₹1,309.18 (A's profit share) = ₹16,309.18

After transferring ₹16,309.18 to A, B retains:

₹1,309.18 (B's profit) + ₹654.59 (tax) = ₹1,963.77

Verification: ₹16,309.18 + ₹1,963.77 = ₹18,272.95 ≈ ₹15,000 + ₹3,272.94 = ₹18,272.94 (1-paisa rounding)
